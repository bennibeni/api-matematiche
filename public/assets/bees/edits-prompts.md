## queen-large.png

Edit this queen honeybee illustration. Preserve golden naturalistic style,
dorsal pose, transparent background, full bee. Make her abdomen unmistakably
larger, longer and fuller, extending prominently beyond the wing tips, about
twice thorax length. Broad upper abdomen tapering to rounded point, not skinny.
Slightly shorten wings relative to abdomen. Queen anatomy, no pollen baskets.
Frame entire bee with small equal margins; no text.
